BizTrack Pro — Business Tracker (English)

A clean, powerful business management web app for small business owners — track sales, expenses, and profit with zero setup.

✨ Features
💰 Sales & Revenue tracking — log every transaction
📉 Expense categorization — know where money goes
📦 Inventory management — stock in, stock out
📊 Profit & Loss dashboard — visual, real-time
📓 Business diary — notes and observations
🌙 Dark & Light mode
📱 Mobile-first design — works on any device
💾 No account needed — all data stored locally
